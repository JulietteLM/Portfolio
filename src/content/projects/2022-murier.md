## Conception of a video game for expressing art in an inclusive way

Participation to the initial phase of conception of the video game during an Erasmus at UQAC Univeristy (Chicoutimi, Canada)

This project investigates how to deliberately integrate women's personal experiences into game design practices by developing a 3D poetic video game centered on female bodily pleasure, aiming to enrich and transform design methodologies for more inclusive representation.

More information on [_this page_](https://www.uqac.ca/praxis/projets-de-recherche/valoriser-et-transmettre-lexperience-vecue-des-femmes-dans-le-processus-de-design-de-jeux-video/).
