## Creation of an immersive escape game


Creation of an immersive escape game during a conception of video games course in UQAC Univeristy (Chicoutimi, Canada)

In this video game, you have to find the correct solution to leave each room and go to the next one, but be carefull, and don't forget the butterfly effect, your actions may have more consequences than what you think. There is a total of 5 escape rooms in this game and 4 different endings. To complete the game you must find every endings.
